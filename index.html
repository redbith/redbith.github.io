CVE-2026-16478

pardus-nvidia-installer — Permissive List of Allowed Inputs

A permissive allow-list flaw in the Pardus NVIDIA installer, developed by TÜBİTAK BİLGEM, allows local privilege escalation in versions before 2.0.1. Vector: CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:H/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H. Announced as an official advisory by the Presidency of Türkiye’s Cybersecurity Directorate.
